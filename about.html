01/ About

From storyboards to servers.

We’re 3w, a web studio in Dublin founded by Alex Shanley. Design, build and everything that keeps a site running afterwards sit in one place here, rather than being passed between four.

02/ The route
  1. 01

    Motion picture arts

    Wolverhampton University

    Storyboards, sequence and timing. What to show first, what to hold back, and how long a moment needs before it lands.

  2. 02

    Graphic design

    Griffith College Dublin

    Typography, hierarchy and grid. The craft of telling somebody where to look without ever telling them.

  3. 03

    Full stack development

    UCD Professional Academy

    Everything that happens after the design is signed off, which turned out to be the part most studios quietly hand to somebody else.

So we kept going. The unglamorous half of the job now takes as much of the week as the drawing does, and it is the half that decides whether a site is still standing in two years.
