01/ About

From storyboards to servers.

I’m Alex Shanley. I started in motion graphics, moved through graphic design, and ended up writing the code and running the servers. 3w is what happens when all of that sits with one person instead of four.

Most people who build websites come from one side of the work. I came through both, the long way round.

A motion picture arts degree taught me sequence and timing. A graphic design degree taught me typography and hierarchy. Then a full stack diploma taught me what actually happens after the design is signed off, and that turned out to be the part most studios quietly hand to somebody else.

So I kept going. These days I spend as much time in DNS records, staging environments and SSL certificates as I do in Figma. It is not the glamorous half of the job, but it is the half that decides whether a site is still standing in two years.
