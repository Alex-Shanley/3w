04/ Track record
In practice

Alongside 3w, Alex works as Head of Web Development at Automate Technology and leads product delivery at Voucher Connect, two roles that run in parallel and feed directly into how this studio operates.

That work covers responsive websites for 20+ clients across financial services, mining and corporate sectors: a Central Bank of Ireland regulated platform with conditional-routing lead capture built to Core Web Vitals benchmarks, and an investor-ready CMS rebuild carrying real-time copper charts, automated dual-feed news and live LSE share prices at zero licensing cost.

On the product side, it means shipping a cross-platform iOS and Android app that replaced proprietary point-of-sale hardware, sub-30-second transaction loops built on native QR scanning, offline resilience and PCI-DSS compliant payments, integrated with Flipdish, Phorest, WooCommerce and Zapier, plus domain migrations and DNS configuration for high-traffic sites with no loss of search rankings.

Design and engineering in the same pair of hands.
