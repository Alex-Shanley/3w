A dental practice that books itself

Oak Lane Dental — full homepage screenshot, desktop
Challenge

A busy practice, invisible online

Oak Lane is a three-dentist practice in Sheffield with a full appointment book — built entirely on referrals. Their old site loaded in nine seconds, didn't work on phones, and sat on page four for "dentist Sheffield". New patients couldn't find them, and the front desk spent two hours a day booking by phone.

The brief was plain: be found, and let people book without calling.

Approach

Pages built around treatments people search for

We rebuilt the site around fourteen treatment pages — one per service patients actually search for, each answering cost, pain and recovery questions upfront. Booking sits one tap from every page, wired into their existing practice software.

The site launched in six weeks on 3w hosting, with security and monitoring included. Load time went from nine seconds to 0.8.

Result

Position three in five months

"Dentist Sheffield" moved from page four to position three. Online bookings now handle 42% of new appointments, and the practice has taken on a fourth dentist to meet demand. The front desk got its two hours back.
