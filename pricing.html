02/ How quoting works
One number, up front
Fixed price, fixed scope. You get the figure in writing before any work starts, and if the scope changes we quote the change before we build it.
Two working days
That is how long a quote takes once you have sent a brief. No discovery call required first, though you are welcome to one.
Year one, then your call
Hosting is included for the first year on every build. After that, sites either move onto a Care plan or we hand everything over cleanly. There is no lock-in either way.
