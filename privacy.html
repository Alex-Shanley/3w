Privacy policy.

This page explains what happens to your data when you use the contact form on this website. It does not cover any data handling we do once you become a client — that's set out in your project agreement.

What we collect

When you submit the contact form, we collect the name, email address, company name, budget range and project description you enter. We don't use cookies or analytics scripts on this site, so we don't collect any browsing data alongside it.

Why we collect it

Solely to respond to your enquiry, assess whether we're a good fit for your project, and send you a quote.

Our lawful basis

We process this data under legitimate interest — replying to a direct enquiry you've sent us — and, by submitting the form, your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us; see below.

Who sees it

Your enquiry is sent to us via Formspree, a third-party form processor, and then to our email inbox. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port the data we hold on you, and to object to how we use it. To exercise any of these, email hello@3w.studio. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie) if you're unhappy with how we've handled your data.
