A luxury tour that finally looks like one.

A complete visual redesign for a second-generation family-run luxury chauffeur experience through County Wicklow — seven stops, a €750 private minivan, and TripAdvisor reviews from across Europe and North America.

01 Challenge

The Avoca Wicklow Day Tour is a genuinely exceptional product — a private chauffeured journey through some of Ireland's most photographed landscapes, run by a family with decades of local knowledge and glowing reviews in five languages. The original website told a very different story.

Cluttered, visually inconsistent and built on a generic template, it actively undercut the experience it represented. At the premium end of Irish tourism — where a guest is choosing between this and a luxury hotel experience — the website is the first and most critical trust signal.

The digital presence has to match the physical experience.

Full-page captures. Scroll each panel to compare.

02 Approach

A single cohesive dark navy identity with warm gold and linen replaced multiple competing logos and widgets. Every section now leads with full-bleed landscape photography, letting Powerscourt, Glendalough and Guinness Lake do the selling instead of stock-style icons.

The €750 price moved from buried text to a prominent anchor — a counterintuitive but deliberate choice that filters in serious bookers and signals the experience justifies the investment.

Multilingual TripAdvisor reviews were woven into the page flow at the point of maximum hesitation, rather than left sitting in the footer.

03 Result

A guest spending €750 on a private luxury day out now sees a site that belongs to the same world as the leather seats and the guide who knows every stone in Glendalough.

A Bespoke Luxury Tours section presents six further experiences — Cliffs of Moher, Belfast, Kerry, Whiskey, Golf and Dublin City — turning a single booking into a repeat relationship.

Delivered
  • Unified dark navy, gold and linen palette
  • Full-viewport cinematic hero
  • €750 anchored as a confidence signal
  • Multilingual TripAdvisor reviews in the page flow
  • All seven tour stops with dedicated imagery
  • Ford Tourneo, Mercedes E Class and V Class with pricing
  • Six Bespoke Luxury Tours as a curated collection
  • Concierge form with US Toll Free, Irish mobile and WhatsApp
