Institutional trust, built to comply.

A high-performance, fully compliant corporate web presence for one of Ireland’s leading award-winning financial brokerages — regulated by the Central Bank of Ireland.

01 Challenge

Engage Financial Solutions is a Central Bank of Ireland regulated brokerage operating from Blackrock, Co. Dublin, providing mortgages, life and income protection, retirement planning, wealth management, savings and investments to both individual and business clients.

The work went well beyond template implementation: restructuring the entire CMS architecture from the ground up, engineering multi-step conversion funnels for each service vertical, and holding strict GDPR and Central Bank compliance across every digital data collection point.

Compliance had to be structural, not bolted on afterwards.

02 Approach

The site is architected around a deep service tree that had to stay navigable for both a first-time buyer exploring a mortgage and an experienced investor managing a retirement portfolio.

Mortgages, insurance, financial planning, retirement, and savings and investment each carry their own landing page and tailored conversion pathway. A first-time buyer lands on a page built specifically for their situation, while a business owner researching group protection takes an entirely different and contextually appropriate journey.

03 Result

A platform that performs as an authoritative institutional presence while remaining effortlessly navigable at every financial life stage, with an integrated online application flow.

Data hierarchy, trust signals and user flow were all shaped by what a regulated brokerage is required to demonstrate, rather than retrofitted once the design was settled.

Delivered
  • Complete CMS architecture restructure
  • Dedicated landing page and funnel per service vertical
  • Mortgages: first-time buyers, moving, switching, self-build, buy-to-let
  • Insurance: mortgage protection, life, income, critical illness, group
  • Financial planning: tax, inheritance, wealth management
  • Retirement: annuities, ARFs, PRSAs, company and personal pensions
  • GDPR and Central Bank compliance at every collection point
  • Integrated online application flow
