ClientThe Northbrook Clinic IndustrySpecialist medical & dental LocationRanelagh, Dublin 6 ScopeServer migration & DNS

Moved servers without going offline.

Summary

A server migration and DNS management engagement for one of Dublin’s established specialist medical and dental clinics — founded in 1993, referral-based, with eight specialists across dental and medical disciplines.

Challenge

The clinic's website is the primary information and contact point for patients seeking referral-based specialist care across eight clinical disciplines, from oral surgery and dental implantology to psychiatric and reconstructive surgery. For a healthcare provider of this standing, uninterrupted availability is not a convenience — it is a clinical accessibility requirement.

The work was a full migration of their WordPress site off an ageing hosting environment onto a new server, with the corresponding DNS updates, and no disruption to patient-facing availability at any point.

Approach

Server migrations carry specific risks that are amplified in a healthcare context. A full WordPress database export, transfer and import had to land every specialist profile, service page and department listing intact and uncorrupted.

DNS changes were scoped carefully to leave the clinic's MX records untouched, so patient and referral email continued uninterrupted throughout. A valid SSL certificate was issued and configured on the new server before cutover, so the site never resolved insecurely.

Result

The site moved to its new environment with no loss of availability and clean domain resolution after cutover, with no propagation window where the site appeared offline.

Patients kept access to specialist profiles, services and contact details throughout — which, for a referral-based clinic, is the entire point of the exercise.

What was delivered
  • Full WordPress file and database migration
  • Zero-downtime cutover planning
  • DNS record updates with clean propagation
  • MX records preserved for uninterrupted email
  • SSL certificate issued and configured before cutover
  • Post-migration verification of all site content
