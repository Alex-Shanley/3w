ClientThe Northbrook Clinic IndustrySpecialist medical & dental LocationRanelagh, Dublin 6 ScopeServer migration & DNS

Moved servers without going offline.

Founded in 1993 and referral-based, with eight specialists across dental and medical disciplines, and a site that had to stay reachable throughout.

Visit the live site →northbrookclinic.ie
01 The problem

The clinic's website is the primary information and contact point for patients seeking referral-based specialist care across eight clinical disciplines, from oral surgery and dental implantology to psychiatric and reconstructive surgery.

The work was a full migration of their WordPress site off an ageing hosting environment onto a new server, with the corresponding DNS updates, and no disruption to patient-facing availability at any point.

Uninterrupted availability is a clinical requirement, not a convenience.

02 Core challenge areas

Migration risks are amplified in a healthcare context, where downtime directly affects patient access.

Zero downtime

Any interruption could prevent patients reaching specialist profiles, services or contact information, unacceptable in a clinical setting.

Database integrity

A full WordPress database export, transfer and import, ensuring every specialist profile and service page arrived intact.

DNS propagation

Poorly timed record updates can cause resolution failures during propagation, creating a window where the site appears offline globally.

Email continuity

DNS changes scoped to leave MX records untouched, so patient and referral communications were unaffected throughout.

SSL transfer

A valid certificate issued and configured on the new server before cutover, so the site never resolved insecurely.

Verification

Post-migration checks across all content, confirming nothing was lost or corrupted in transfer.

03 Outcome

The site moved to its new environment with no loss of availability and clean domain resolution after cutover, with no propagation window where it appeared offline.

Patients kept access to specialist profiles, services and contact details throughout, which, for a referral-based clinic, is the entire point of the exercise.
