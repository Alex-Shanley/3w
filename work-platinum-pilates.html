One site for five studios and three clinics.

Summary

A complete ground-up Squarespace build for Ireland's leading reformer Pilates and physiotherapy brand — class timetables, tiered pricing, online booking, an e-commerce shop and gift cards, all from a single cohesive presence.

Challenge

Platinum Pilates had grown into Ireland's leading reformer Pilates and physiotherapy brand, with five studios and three physiotherapy clinics across Dublin — Sandymount, Stillorgan, Clonskeagh, Glenageary and Hatch Street. As the business scaled, it needed a platform matching both its premium positioning and its operational complexity.

The site had to speak to four audiences at once: newcomers trying Pilates for the first time, existing clients managing bookings and class packs, physiotherapy patients seeking specialist care, and corporate partners looking at workplace wellness.

Approach

Dedicated studio pages for all five locations, each with its own timetable links and location-specific detail, sit under a single brand-led design. The class structure covers four reformer levels — Essentials, Dynamic 1, 2 and 3 — plus Reformer Natal, Clinical Pilates, Tower Pilates and Corporate Wellness.

The whole booking layer integrates with Mindbody: class booking, timetables, account login and registration, and class pack purchasing, all embedded inside the Squarespace environment rather than bolted on beside it.

Result

A tiered pricing page presents intro offers, class packs and memberships in a structure designed to convert first-time visitors, while an online shop handles merchandise and class pack sales with Mindbody gift card integration.

Physiotherapy runs as its own section covering general physio, Clinical Pilates and Emsella Chair treatment, with dedicated pages for each service and each of the three clinic locations.

What was delivered
  • Brand-led Squarespace design across all pages
  • Dedicated pages for five studio locations
  • Four reformer class tiers plus Natal, Clinical and Tower Pilates
  • Physiotherapy section covering three therapy disciplines
  • Full Mindbody booking, timetable and membership integration
  • E-commerce shop with Mindbody gift card purchase and redemption
  • Tiered pricing architecture for intro offers, packs and memberships
  • App Store and Google Play deep links
